About Dr. Mediha Tair

A practice built on detail, discretion and trust.

Dr. Mediha Tair is an orthodontist whose practice is shaped by an unwavering attention to detail and a deeply personal approach to care. With advanced training in clear-aligner therapy, lingual and ceramic braces, and aesthetic finishing techniques, she designs treatment plans that respect your face, your time and your individuality.

At Smile by Medi, every consultation begins with listening. Each step is considered. Every detail is finished by hand — because a confident smile is, ultimately, a quiet form of luxury.

The Experience

Considered, from the first hello.

Treatment at Smile by Medi unfolds across four unhurried chapters — each designed to give you clarity, comfort and confidence.

  1. 01

    Welcome

    A private consultation, an espresso, and an honest conversation about what you'd like to change.

  2. 02
