About Dr. Mediha Tair-Gyuryeeva

A practice built on detail, discretion and trust.

Dr. Mediha Tair-Gyuryeeva is a dental medicine specialist and orthodontist dedicated to creating beautiful, healthy smiles using contemporary orthodontic technologies and methods. She established her private dental practice in 2021, driven by a commitment to providing individualized, modern treatment approaches.

Her professional focus centers on orthodontics, with particular emphasis on aligner treatment for patients across all age groups. With nearly five years of professional experience, she has earned multiple certifications in aesthetic, high-quality composite restorations for visible tooth areas, and completed specialized training in Belgium focused on aesthetic restorations.

"Every smile is unique and deserves an individual approach."

Dr. Tair communicates fluently in Bulgarian, English and Turkish — enabling close, trusting relationships with patients of diverse nationalities. Her practice philosophy is sustained through continuous professional development, specialized training in Bulgaria and abroad, and active membership in the field's leading professional societies.
